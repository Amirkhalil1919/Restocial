Sources used for these estimates: Active Pakistani commercial kitchen suppliers (Pakeeza Commercial Kitchen, Mughal Electric); Pakistan Bureau of Statistics weekly Sensitive Price Indicator for food prices (PBS Official Portal); WageIndicator Pakistan for labour pay ranges (Cooks, Minimum Wage). Equipment prices not tracked by PBS or WageIndicator are estimated from local market research, since Pakistan's commercial kitchen equipment trade is mostly informal and quote-based. Treat every number here as a planning starting point, not a fixed quote — confirm with your own suppliers before committing money.
